Key insight

Semantic depth is structurally isomorphic to spectral band

Lower LLM layers encode fine-grained, local lexical detail; higher layers encode abstract, global macro-semantics. In market data the same hierarchy appears along frequency: high-frequency components carry short-lived micro-shocks, low-frequency components carry long-horizon macro-trends. Fin-CoDiT makes the two hierarchies explicitly address each other, so a macro instruction cannot leak into a one-day jump and a shock cannot smear across a quarter.

Method

Three components, each answering one hard constraint

Unconditional generators can only add samples; they cannot simulate a deliberate market dynamics. Conditioning a diffusion transformer on market prose fails for two structural reasons — cross-variable physical constraints and frequency-addressable control. Fin-CoDiT fixes both inside the network topology.

01 — TF-CVAE

Time-Frequency Causal VAE

A channel-level bottleneck with no spatial downsampling (a downsample would alias frequencies and leak the future). An orthogonal causal encoder runs convolutions along the time axis with left padding only; a Local-Queried Attention bottleneck compresses four latent channels. Decoding is branched: a trend head emits open/close, a volatility head emits shadow extensions, and a differentiable morphological projection composes them so that High ≥ max(O, C) and Low ≤ min(O, C) hold unconditionally.

CVR 0.05% leak R² 0.01 no-SFA 0.62

02 — RoutDiT

Grouped Routing Attention

Square patchification is abandoned: tokens are cut as PT × 1 pure time windows inside one frequency band, and positional encoding becomes PEtime(t′) + PEfreq(f). Attention heads are partitioned into low / mid / high groups that share one frequency-specific Query, and each group performs a Top-K sparse selection of LLM layers under an explicit frequency prior. A prior-preservation KL term on the dense pre-truncation logits keeps the routing distribution anchored.

Top-K 5 / 32 SCS 0.892 Event F1 0.854

03 — Data workflow

Multi-agent synthesis

Real news and prices cannot be aligned directly. Price segments are profiled into a quantitative state, news is distilled by FinBERT-MRC into an event state, and a local Qwen3.5-397B-A17B agent writes the market prose. A hard factual guardrail, a semantic de-duplication pass and a back-translation consistency check keep only pairs whose text survives re-reading — Gold (C ≥ 0.85), Silver (0.70 ≤ C < 0.85) and rejected.

Zero-shot stress test

Counterfactual scenarios the model has never seen

Synthetic prompts describe extreme events that do not exist in the training set. The test is not whether the series looks plausible, but whether the event lands on the right day.

S1Geopolitical escalation & oil shock

Bond futures plunge −12.4% cumulative over 7 days; a Day-2 oil supply shock opens a gap-down and a −6.5% intraday drawdown through the 101.50 support on 2.8× volume.

Fin-CoDiTDay-2 gap-down reproduced exactly, followed by realistic post-event consolidation.
T2SSmooths the sharp liquidation into a monotonic curve.
S2Cooling inflation & short squeeze

Treasury futures post +5.6% over 7 days; a surprise −0.5% MoM CPI print on Day 5 sparks a +3.8% single-day surge that clears the 112.80 resistance on 2.2× volume.

Fin-CoDiTDay-5 CPI breakout reproduced exactly, with the volatility regime retained.
T2SRenders the step-like surge as a gradual ramp.
S3Mid-week hawkish trend reversal

Bond futures round out a volatile 7-day cycle after a sharp mid-week trend reversal: prices break lower, and hawkish commentary on Day 4 triggers a severe −4.7% liquidation and a −5.2% maximum drawdown from the peak.

Fin-CoDiTDay-4 hawkish reversal reproduced; the regime boundary lands on the specified day.
T2SMisses the regime boundary entirely.
S4Abrupt liquidity / banking-default shock

A sudden liquidity event produces a Day-3 gap and a deep intraday drawdown that the trend structure alone cannot express.

Fin-CoDiTDay-3 banking-default liquidation reproduced exactly.
T2SSmooths the sharp liquidation into a monotonic curve.

Human evaluation corroborates the visual result: Fin-CoDiT scores 3.45 ± 0.15 against T2S's 3.12 ± 0.21 on a 5-point Likert scale.

Market profiling

Sliding windows become a quantitative state — trend, volatility, extreme events, technical patterns, volume profiles — merged with a FinBERT-MRC event state extracted from Wind news.

Scene description

A locally deployed Qwen3.5-397B-A17B agent turns each market state into 15 diverse natural-language market descriptions.

Filtering & de-duplication

A hard factual alignment guardrail removes directional and numerical contradictions; embedding similarity above 0.90 drops redundant variants.

Back-translation check

The quality verifier re-reads each description and reconstructs a market state; consistency C splits pairs into Gold (≥ 0.85), Silver (0.70–0.85) and rejected.
